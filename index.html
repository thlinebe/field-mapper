    How to get a location from Google / Apple Maps

    Google Maps (computer): right-click the spot → click the coordinates at the top of the menu (copies them) → paste here.
    Google Maps (phone): long-press to drop a pin → swipe up → tap the coordinates to copy.
    Apple Maps (Mac): right-click → Drop Pin → click the pin → copy the coordinates shown, or Share → Copy Link.
    Apple Maps (iPhone): long-press to drop a pin → swipe up → tap the coordinates to copy.
    Short share links (maps.app.goo.gl…, maps.apple/p/…) can't be read directly — open the link first, then copy the coordinates or the full address-bar link.

    Tip: on a computer you can also drag a photo or link anywhere onto the map, or press Ctrl/⌘+V to paste a link. Pins are temporary (this device only) — they clear when you close the app.
